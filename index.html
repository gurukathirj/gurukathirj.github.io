Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

RetroCam Sync ("the app", "we", "our") converts legacy camcorder video files (such as AVCHD/.MTS clips) into standard MP4 so they can be viewed, shared, and preserved. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

The short version

Your videos are converted entirely on your device. We do not upload, view, or store your media on any server, and the app has no user accounts, no login, and no advertising.

Two things do leave the device, and only ever to Google: crash and error reports (so we can fix bugs) and anonymous usage analytics (so we can see which features people use). Neither is linked to your identity, neither is used to track you across apps or websites, and neither contains your media, file paths, or file contents. You can read the details below.

Information We Access

  • Photos Library. With your permission, the app saves the converted MP4 files back to your Photos library. The app requests "Add Photos Only" access — it never reads your existing photo or video library.
  • Folders you choose. When you pick a camcorder card or folder, the app reads the video files inside it on your device. iOS grants access only to the folder you select (and only for as long as the app needs it). The app never browses your files without you choosing them.
  • File metadata. The app reads timestamps, duration, and other container metadata from the clips you select, and generates thumbnail images for the in-app feed. This is used only for in-app display and for tagging the converted file with its original recording date.
  • Location you assign. If you optionally place a map pin on a clip, the app uses Apple's search and geocoding services to turn a place name into coordinates. The app does not request access to your device's live location. Pins are stored locally on your device and embedded into the converted file's metadata if you save it to Photos.
  • App preferences. Settings such as onboarding status, conversion counters, and remembered folders are stored locally on your device using standard iOS app storage. This data never leaves your device.

Crash & Diagnostic Reporting

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google) to automatically report crashes and non-fatal errors so we can fix bugs. These reports:

  • Are not linked to your identity — no account ID, device ID, name, or email is attached.
  • Are not used for tracking you across apps or websites.
  • Never include your videos, photo files, or full file paths. At most they include a clip's display name (for example, CLIP0001.MTS) and the error code shown in the app.
  • Are limited to things like: crash logs, app version, OS version, and performance timings.

Reports are sent when the device has a network connection. The app works fully offline either way.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record anonymous, aggregate usage events — which screens are opened and which buttons are tapped (for example, "convert", "save to Photos"). These events:

  • Are not linked to your identity.
  • Are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • Do not contain your media, file paths, or file contents.

Both Crashlytics and Analytics are products of Google LLC. You can read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

In-App Purchases

RetroCam Sync offers an optional one-time unlock via Apple's StoreKit. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details, billing address, or card information.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't require account creation or login.
  • We don't show third-party ads.
  • We don't sell or rent your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't upload your videos or photos to any server, ours or anyone else's.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Children's Privacy

RetroCam Sync is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention

Because the app does not operate a server or store your media remotely, there is nothing for us to retain on your behalf. Your converted videos and thumbnails live in the app's storage on your device and can be deleted at any time from the app's Settings screen or by deleting the app. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google according to its own retention policy (see the Google privacy policy above).

Your Choices

  • Delete converted files and cached thumbnails at any time in Settings → Storage & Diagnostics.
  • Revoke Photos access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.
